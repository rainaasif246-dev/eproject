About EduPredict
Built for the future of
AI Education

EduPredict combines Hadoop Big Data infrastructure with Machine Learning to help institutions make data-driven decisions and improve student outcomes.

Our Mission

Turning student data into actionable insights

EduPredict is an AI-powered analytics platform designed to help educational institutions monitor student performance, detect dropout risks early, and forecast course demand — all powered by Hadoop's distributed computing and machine learning.

From predicting exam scores to detecting at-risk students before they drop out — EduPredict empowers administrators, teachers, and analysts with the tools they need to act fast.

How It Works
From raw data to actionable insights
Four simple steps to transform educational data into predictions.
1
Ingest Data
Upload academic records, attendance, LMS logs into HDFS via batch or real-time Kafka stream.
2
Process & Store
Spark & MapReduce process data, stored in partitioned HDFS directories for fast retrieval.
3
ML Prediction
Random Forest models analyze 21 features to predict exam scores and detect at-risk students.
4
Alert & Act
Automated alerts notify stakeholders. Teachers intervene before students drop out.
