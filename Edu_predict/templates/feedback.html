Frequently Asked Questions
Quick answers to common questions
How are exam scores predicted? ▼
EduPredict uses Linear Regression and Random Forest models trained on 6,607 student records. Features include study hours, attendance, previous scores, parental involvement, and tutoring sessions.
What does the dropout risk score mean? ▼
A score above 60% indicates critical dropout risk requiring immediate intervention. 30–60% is moderate risk, and below 30% is low risk. Scores are calculated from attendance, grades, study hours, and support factors.
Who can access the Data Ingestion page? ▼
Only Administrators and Analysts have access to Data Ingestion, Storage, and Real-Time pages. Teachers and Students have restricted access as per role-based access control (RBAC).
How often are ML models retrained? ▼
Models are retrained automatically when new batch data is ingested into HDFS. The system maintains model versioning and logs R² scores after each retraining cycle.
